Which meal kit should you choose for your Christmas menu in 2026?

Which meal kit for a Christmas 2026 menu? Our comparison of the boxes that deliver a complete festive meal: menus, number of guests, prep time and cost per plate.

By Benjamin Bourgoin
  1. Home
  2. Comparison
  3. Best meal kit for batch cooking

A Christmas meal is won as much on organisation as on recipes. Finding a coherent festive menu, sizing quantities for six or eight guests, shopping in stores under siege: that is where the evening is made or lost. A meal kit answers exactly those three points, delivering ready-built menus and pre-measured ingredients. What remains is knowing which ones genuinely offer a festive range, and what they are worth once at the table.

Les Commis follows closely on the gastronomic side. For a Christmas Eve with no cooking at all, ready meals such as Kitchen Daily will do, but the spirit of a festive meal is lost.

Why a meal kit works well for a festive meal

A Christmas meal concentrates three difficulties you do not meet the rest of the year.

The first is building the menu. A starter, main and dessert sequence that holds together, with compatible cooking times and a single oven, takes real planning. Boxes deliver menus already designed as a whole, which no list of separately gathered recipes guarantees.

The second is sizing quantities. Going from four to eight guests is not simple multiplication, especially on cooking times and sauces. Ingredients weighed for a precise number of covers remove the guesswork, and with it the end-of-meal waste.

The third is the calendar. Shopping on 23 December is an ordeal. A scheduled delivery solves it, provided you order in time, and that is precisely where most people get caught.

How to choose your box for a Christmas meal

Number of guests, first of all

This is the criterion that rules out fastest. A table of six or eight immediately closes the door on single-portion boxes and on those capped at four covers. Check this first, before even looking at the menus.

The order deadline

This is the most frequent and most costly mistake. Delivery slots for Christmas week fill up well before December, and a festive box ordered on 20 December has little chance of arriving in time. Look at the deadline shown as soon as the range opens, usually in November, and book then.

The real difficulty level

A festive menu that assumes three simultaneous cooking times in one oven will not work. Boxes that state a prep time and a difficulty level per recipe let you plan ahead, where a magazine menu saves the surprise for the day itself.

Cost per plate

Cost per plate decreases with volume, which favours large tables. Beware of welcome offers reserved for a first order: to compare, look at the recurring price excluding promotions. Our guide how much does a meal kit cost gives benchmarks per person and per month.

Frequently asked questions

Which meal kit should you choose for a Christmas menu in 2026?

Quitoque is the best placed: it is the only cook-yourself box with a dedicated range of Christmas menus, as starter, main and dessert, with portions calibrated from two to six guests and seasonal produce mostly of French origin. Prices start from EUR 3.83 per plate on the larger plans. Les Commis is the most gastronomic alternative, at a higher price point.

Can you order a Christmas box for 6 or 8 people?

Up to six guests, yes, with cook-yourself boxes such as Quitoque or Les Commis. Beyond six, the usual practice is to order two formats and combine the menus, which stays easier to manage than scaling up recipes found separately. Single-portion boxes are not suitable for a festive table.

What is the deadline to order a box and be delivered before Christmas Eve?

Deadlines are much earlier than people expect, because delivery slots for Christmas week fill up fast. In practice the festive range opens in November and the best slots go within days. The exact deadline is shown on the offer page: that is the first thing to check, before even choosing the menu.

How long does it take to prepare a Christmas menu with a meal kit?

Far less than an improvised menu, because shopping and planning disappear. What remains is the cooking itself, which varies by menu and usually runs between one and two hours for a full meal. The point of a box is precisely that this time is stated in advance, recipe by recipe, which no food-press menu guarantees.

Does a Christmas meal kit cost more than cooking from scratch?

Not necessarily. For a festive meal, buying small quantities of premium ingredients at retail is expensive and generates leftovers. A box delivers the right quantities at a price known in advance, from EUR 3.83 per plate at Quitoque on the larger plans. The real gap comes from avoided waste rather than the sticker price.
