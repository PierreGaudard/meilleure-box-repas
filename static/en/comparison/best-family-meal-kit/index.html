Best meal kit for a family: which one should you choose?

Which meal kit should a family choose? Our comparison of the boxes built for households: quantities, recipes children actually eat, tapered prices and flexibility.

By Benjamin Bourgoin
  1. Home
  2. Comparison
  3. Best family meal kit

Feeding a whole household every evening is a different exercise from cooking for one. You need quantities that actually fill everyone, recipes children will not push around the plate, and a price per plate that stays reasonable once you multiply by four. We compared the main meal kits on exactly that.

In short

Quitoque is our best family meal kit. It goes up to 6-person formats, the price per plate tapers as the volume grows, and the recipes stay simple enough for children while keeping seasonal produce and French sourcing. Les Commis follows on quality, HelloFresh on the sheer size of its catalogue. Ready meals are individual portions, so they suit families much less well.

What makes a good family meal kit

Three things matter and none of them is the marketing promise. The quantities first: a box announced for 4 people must genuinely feed two adults and two children. Then the recipes, which need to stay readable for a child's palate without becoming bland for the adults. Finally the price, which only makes sense per plate: most boxes taper it as the number of portions grows, so a family often pays less per plate than a couple.

Quitoque: the best meal kit for a family

Quitoque is the box that scales best. Formats run from 2 to 6 people and the price per plate tapers with the volume, which matters when you order for four every week. The recipes stay accessible, ready in under 30 minutes, with 44 new ones every week so nobody gets bored, and 90% rated Nutri-Score A or B for parents who watch that. Produce is seasonal, fruit and vegetables mostly of French origin, meat 100% French. Portions arrive measured, which limits both waste and arguments about who got more. More in our Quitoque review.

Les Commis also goes up to 6-person formats with generous portions and chef-signed recipes. It is a good fit for households where the adults want something a bit more interesting than a weeknight staple, as long as the children follow. The price positioning is higher, which weighs more once multiplied by four. More in our Les Commis review.

HelloFresh: the big choice to keep varying

HelloFresh caps at 4-person formats, which excludes larger households, but its catalogue is by far the widest. For a family with fussy eaters that is a real argument: you will always find something everyone accepts. Entry prices are aggressive, sourcing less seasonal and less French. Seazon, Aussitôt Bon, Kitchen Daily and Cheef: individual portions

These four deliver chilled ready meals in individual portions. For a household that means ordering one dish per person per meal, which gets expensive fast and removes the shared table. They remain useful as a backup for a busy evening, or for a teenager who eats at a different time, but they are not a family solution. How to choose a family meal kit

Beyond the ranking, four checks are worth doing before you subscribe.

Count the real portions

A 4-person format does not always feed two adults and two hungry teenagers. Read the grammage per portion rather than the number of people, and if in doubt order one size up for the first delivery.

Check the price per plate, not the basket

Boxes taper the price as the volume grows, so a family usually pays less per plate than a couple. Compare on the recurring price excluding welcome offers, otherwise the first basket misleads you.

Involve the children in the choice

Most boxes let you pick your recipes each week. Letting a child choose one dish out of four does more for a peaceful table than any nutritional argument.

Keep the flexibility

School holidays, a week away, a busy period: check you can pause or skip a week without friction before committing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best meal kit for a family?

Quitoque is the best suited to a household: formats from 2 to 6 people, a price per plate that tapers with the volume, recipes ready in under 30 minutes and simple enough for children, with seasonal produce and French sourcing. Les Commis is a good qualitative alternative, and HelloFresh wins on the size of the catalogue if your children are hard to please.

Is a meal kit worth it for a family?

It depends what you compare it with. Against takeaway or delivery it is clearly cheaper. Against a well planned supermarket shop it usually costs more per plate, but you remove the menu planning, the shopping and most of the food waste, since portions arrive measured.

Do children eat the recipes from meal kits?

Broadly yes, provided you choose the recipes rather than take the default selection. Most boxes let you swap dishes each week, so you can steer towards formats children accept, pasta, gratins, breaded dishes, and keep the more adventurous recipes for the adults.

Which meal kit works for a large family?

Above four people the choice narrows: Quitoque and Les Commis go up to 6-person formats, HelloFresh stops at 4. For a very large household, ordering a 6-person format and cooking one extra side dish is usually more economical than doubling a smaller box.
