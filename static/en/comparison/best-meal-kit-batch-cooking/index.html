Batch cooking and meal kits: why they work well together

Batch cooking means cooking the base of every weeknight dinner in a single session, usually at the weekend. The principle is simple, but two steps put people off fast: planning coherent menus and buying the right quantities without waste. That is exactly what a cook-yourself meal kit removes. You receive recipes designed for the week and fresh ingredients already measured, which leaves you only the enjoyable part: cooking.

Not every box performs equally here. A cook-yourself box genuinely lets you batch cook, with raw produce to prepare and spread across several meals. Ready meals delivered in batches are not batch cooking in the strict sense: you are not cooking, you are stocking meals to reheat. It is a valid alternative when time is short, but the spirit is different.

Quitoque: the box built to cook your whole week

Quitoque is our benchmark for batch cooking without losing your Sunday. The box delivers fresh ingredients already measured to the gram and a step-by-step recipe card, which removes the two chores of batch cooking: deciding the menu and doing the shopping. You can then prepare several meals in a row from shared bases, with no waste. The range offers 44 new recipes every week, two of them reworked by chef Philippe Etchebest, with seasonal produce, fruit and vegetables mostly of French origin and 100% French meat. Recipes are ready in under 30 minutes and 90% score Nutri-Score A or B. Above all, Quitoque offers a dedicated batch cooking range, with menus designed to prepare the week in one session. HelloFresh: the big catalogue to scale up

HelloFresh remains the best known cook-yourself box, with a huge choice of recipes and very low entry prices. For batch cooking, its strength is volume: it is easy to find simple recipes to prepare in quantity and spread across several meals. Sourcing is less seasonal and less French than at Quitoque, but the price-to-choice ratio is unbeatable to get started. More in our HelloFresh review.

HelloFresh pros

HelloFresh cons

How to choose your box for batch cooking

The right choice depends first on your relationship with cooking and on household size. Here are the criteria that really matter for batch cooking.

Format and portions

To prepare a family's week, aim for a cook-yourself box with formats from 2 to 6 people, such as Quitoque or Les Commis. Ready meals in individual portions suit a single person or office use better.

Measured ingredients and freshness

The point of batch cooking with a box is receiving accurate quantities already weighed, which avoids waste. Boxes built on seasonal produce and local sourcing, such as Quitoque, give better bases to work from.

Cost per plate

Price is best assessed per plate, and is often tapered by the volume ordered. Be wary of welcome offers (free delivery or discounts limited to the first order): to compare, look at the recurring price excluding promotions. Our guide on how much a meal kit costs per month gives benchmarks per person.

Subscription flexibility

Check whether you can pause, skip a week or cancel without commitment. That matters when batch cooking stalls during a busy week.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best meal kit for batch cooking?

Among cook-yourself boxes, Quitoque is the best suited to batch cooking: pre-measured fresh ingredients, 44 new recipes every week and a dedicated batch cooking range to prepare the whole week in one session. Les Commis and HelloFresh are two other solid cook-yourself options depending on your budget and household size.

Can you really batch cook with a meal kit?

Yes. A cook-yourself box supplies pre-measured ingredients and recipe cards, which removes menu planning and grocery shopping, the two longest tasks in batch cooking. You simply group the preparation of several recipes into one session, then store or assemble during the week.

Cook-yourself box or ready meals for the week?

For genuine home batch cooking, choose a cook-yourself box such as Quitoque, with raw produce to prepare and spread out. To have your meals ready without cooking, go for ready meals such as Seazon or Kitchen Daily. The first favours fresh and homemade, the second maximum simplicity.

How much does a meal kit for batch cooking cost?

The price is calculated per plate and is usually tapered by volume: the more portions you order, the less each plate costs. Welcome offers distort the comparison, so look at the recurring price excluding promotions. Check the price per plate on each brand's official site before deciding.
