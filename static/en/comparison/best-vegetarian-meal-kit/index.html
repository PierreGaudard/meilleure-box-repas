Best vegetarian meal kit: which veggie basket should you choose?

Which vegetarian meal kit should you choose? Our comparison of the boxes with the best veggie selection: meat-free recipes, plant proteins, seasonal produce and quality.

By Benjamin Bourgoin
  1. Home
  2. Comparison
  3. Best vegetarian meal kit

Eating vegetarian every day is easy for a week and gets repetitive by the second month. That is exactly where a meal kit helps, provided its veggie selection is wide enough and its plant proteins go beyond the usual lentils. We compared the boxes on the real variety of their meat-free offer.

In short

Quitoque has the best vegetarian selection. Around 40% of its weekly recipes are vegetarian, built on seasonal vegetables and varied plant proteins, which is enough not to eat the same three dishes on a loop. Les Commis follows on quality, HelloFresh on volume. On the ready-meal side, Aussitôt Bon and Cheef offer veggie dishes without cooking.

What makes a good vegetarian meal kit

Two criteria decide everything. The number of veggie recipes per week first, because a box offering two meat-free dishes will bore you within a month. Then the plant proteins used: a selection built only on lentils and chickpeas quickly runs dry, whereas alternating pulses, tofu, cheese, eggs and cereals keeps meals interesting and balanced. Seasonal vegetables do the rest, since a vegetarian dish lives or dies on the quality of its produce.

Quitoque is the box where a vegetarian actually has something to choose from: around 40% of recipes are vegetarian every week, out of 44 new ones, which leaves real room to vary. Which plant proteins in a meal kit

This is what separates a veggie selection you keep from one you drop after a month.

Alternate the sources

Pulses, tofu, eggs, cheese, cereals: rotating them is what keeps a vegetarian week interesting and nutritionally sound. A box that leans only on lentils will bore you fast.

Watch the pulse and cereal pairing

Combining a pulse with a cereal, chickpeas with semolina, beans with rice, gives a more complete amino acid profile. Good boxes do this without making a point of it.

Do not confuse vegetarian with light

A gratin or a veggie curry can be richer than a plain chicken breast. If balance is the goal, look at the Nutri-Score of the recipe rather than the absence of meat.

Check the seasonality

A vegetarian dish rests entirely on its vegetables. Out of season, the same recipe is a different dish, which is why seasonal sourcing weighs more on a veggie box than anywhere else.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best vegetarian meal kit?

Quitoque offers the widest and most seasonal veggie selection, with around 40% of its 44 weekly recipes vegetarian, varied plant proteins and 100% seasonal vegetables. Les Commis is a good qualitative alternative, and HelloFresh offers more veggie recipes in absolute terms thanks to the size of its catalogue, though with less seasonal produce.

Is there a 100% vegetarian meal kit?

Not among the mainstream boxes delivering across France. They all work the same way: a vegetarian selection inside a wider range, which you filter when choosing your recipes each week. In practice a box with around 40% veggie recipes gives you more real choice than a small fully vegetarian range.

Are vegetarian boxes balanced?

They can be, and often more so than an improvised meat-free meal, because the recipes are built to pair a plant protein with a cereal or a dairy product. Look at the Nutri-Score per recipe rather than assuming a dish is light just because it has no meat.

Can you have varied vegetarian recipes every week?

Yes, provided the box has enough of them. With around 40% of 44 weekly recipes, Quitoque leaves room to change dishes for weeks without repeating. Below roughly five veggie recipes a week, repetition sets in quickly.
