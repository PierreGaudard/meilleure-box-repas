What is the best way to get healthy meals delivered?

Meal kits, ready meals, fresh produce boxes: we compare the ways to get healthy meals delivered at home, with the prices recorded and the best option for your situation.

By Benjamin Bourgoin
  1. Home
  2. Comparison
  3. Healthy meal delivery

Getting food delivered and eating well did not use to belong in the same sentence. That has changed. Four families of services now cover healthy meal delivery at home, and they are not aimed at the same people at all. So the real question is not "which service is best", it is "how many minutes am I willing to spend cooking each evening". The answer to that one decides everything else.

The four ways to get healthy meals delivered

They differ on one axis only: what arrives in the box, and therefore what is left to do once the door closes.

The cook-yourself box, the best trade-off

You receive fresh ingredients weighed per portion and illustrated recipe cards. Budget 20 to 40 minutes of cooking. It is the format that keeps the most control over how balanced the meal is, because nothing is processed: you see exactly what goes on the plate. Delivery is weekly, the subscription pauses in two clicks, and you pick your recipes every week from a menu that rotates.

Ready meals delivered

Cooked trays, reheated in 3 to 5 minutes. No cooking, no washing up, a real time saving. The trade-off is double: portions are fixed, and freshness depends on a preservation process (sous vide, pasteurisation, freezing). For a fast meal after a day that ran over, nothing beats it.

The fresh produce box

Fruit, vegetables, sometimes meat and cheese, with no recipes attached. Total freedom, and the obligation to know what to do with it. This format suits people who already cook and are mainly after quality sourcing rather than a planning crutch.

Healthy restaurant delivery

The usual delivery platforms have opened balanced sections, and caterers offer weekly plans in Paris and the larger cities. Handy as a stopgap, but the cost per meal climbs fast and nutritional information is often missing. Keep it for the exception.

What makes the difference day to day

Three very concrete things. First, weighed quantities, which put an end to the leftover cream in the fridge and the half bunch of parsley binned on Sunday. Then regularity: a scheduled delivery mechanically beats a Monday resolution. Finally pleasure, which is not a detail. A food plan dropped after ten days because the dishes are dull is worth nothing, and that is where the generous recipes of a box outlast diet trays.

The limits, because there are some

You have to cook, properly. And on the small plans, two people and two recipes, the price per plate climbs back above EUR 6, which makes the maths less obvious against a well run market stall. Delivery areas cover most of the country but not all of it, so check your postcode before you make plans.

The serious alternatives among cook-yourself boxes

Les Commis plays a more culinary card, with recipes signed by chefs and low GI or low calorie filters. The technical level asked of you is a notch higher, and so is the price, from EUR 4.75 per plate. A good pick for people who enjoy cooking and want to get better at it.

HelloFresh offsets less convincing produce quality with volume: the largest catalogue on the market, "High protein" and "Low carb" tags, and permanent discounts on first orders. Watch the real price once the welcome offer is gone. The head to head is in our Quitoque versus HelloFresh comparison.

When cooking is not an option

Some weeks, cooking is off the table. Three services then take over, with different promises.

Seazon delivers balanced dishes to reheat, designed for lunch at the office as much as for dinner. Convenience is excellent, value for money noticeably less so, at EUR 6 to 9 per dish.

Aussitôt Bon relies on sous-vide cooking at low temperature and an Équilibre range. Dishes reheat in 3 minutes and the texture holds up better than the frozen average.

Cheef speaks to a precise audience, supervised weight loss, with programmes at 1,200 or 1,350 kcal per day and 23% of energy intake from protein to limit muscle loss. It is a protocol, not a year-round way of eating well.

What healthy meal delivery costs

The gap runs from single to double depending on the format. A cook-yourself box works out at EUR 3.83 to 7 per plate, shipping often included above a threshold. Ready meals sit between EUR 4.90 and 9 each. Restaurant delivery easily passes EUR 15 a meal.

The honest calculation compares like for like: a box replaces groceries, not just a meal. For a two-person household cooking four evenings a week, the weekly bill lands around EUR 45 to 60 for eight plates, to be set against an equivalent supermarket basket plus the waste avoided. Traps to avoid

The first is confusing "delivered" with "balanced". A dish delivered hot at 10 pm can be very fatty: the healthy label on a menu commits nobody, only the nutrition detail counts.

The second is the headline price. Almost every brand offers 40 to 60% off the first order. Always compare full prices, because that is what you pay from the second basket onwards.

The third is overestimating your week. Ordering five recipes when you will cook three produces exactly the waste you were trying to avoid. Start at three, scale up later.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best way to get healthy meals delivered?

Quitoque, for most households. It is the cook-yourself box that combines seasonal fresh produce, the full nutrition table on every recipe, portions weighed for two to six people and a price from EUR 3.83 per plate, with no commitment. For anyone who will not cook, Seazon takes over with ready meals.

Can you really eat healthily with delivered meals?

Yes, provided you look at what is published. Cook-yourself boxes have the edge because nothing is processed and quantities are weighed. On the ready meal side, check the values per portion and the ingredient list: that is where excess salt and fat show up.

How can you eat healthily without cooking at all?

Ready meals delivered to the door. Seazon for everyday balance, Aussitôt Bon for cooking quality, Cheef for a weight loss goal. Budget EUR 6 to 9 per dish and accept that portions are no longer yours to set.

Is healthy meal delivery more expensive than grocery shopping?

Not systematically. At comparable produce quality, a cook-yourself box sits at the top of a supermarket basket range, but it removes food waste and impulse buys.
